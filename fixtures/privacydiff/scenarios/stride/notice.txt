Stride Privacy Notice

Information we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and a password. We also collect your age range and gender so we can tailor workouts.

We collect your approximate location, based on your IP address, to show nearby classes. We do not collect precise geolocation.

Our app collects device identifiers and your IP address to keep your account secure and to understand how the app is used.

We may collect health information you choose to enter, such as your weight or heart rate.

How we use it

We use this information to provide the service, send you updates and prevent fraud.
