ShopSmith Privacy Policy

The personal information we collect

We collect identifiers and commercial information when you shop with us. We use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our site.

When you check out we collect your shipping address and payment information. We may also collect your phone number.

If you sign up for our newsletter we collect your email address.

Why we collect it

We use this information to fulfil your orders, for marketing and analytics, and to prevent fraud.
